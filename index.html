☾ lamp_forms.txt
1 · What kind of lamp

Lamp forms

The form decides which way gravity pulls on the joint. That matters more than anything else when choosing a mount.

table lamp
Base sits on a surface, shade rests on top. Joint is in compression; almost any mount works.
bedside / accent lamp
Small table lamp, 120–250 mm tall, usually 2–5 W LED. The most forgiving first project.
mushroom lamp
Wide domed cap on a slim stem. Cap overhangs the base, so it needs a positive lock, not just friction.
pendant
Hangs from the ceiling by its cord. The shade hangs below the socket, so the joint is in tension.
wall sconce
Wall-mounted back plate (keyhole slots) with a shade attached sideways. Joint is in shear.
floor lamp
Tall stem (lamp pipe) plus a weighted base. The shade is often too large for one print and is split into segments.
desk / task lamp
Directed light with an arm or hinge. Uses articulating joints and a cone shade.
lantern
Enclosed shade on all sides, often with a handle or top loop. Good for cord-and-plug or battery LED.
clip-on / clamp lamp
Spring clamp on the base, attaches to a shelf or headboard.
night light / puck light
Low-power LED module or a battery puck instead of a mains socket. Fewest safety constraints.
lithophane lamp
Thin shade panel that shows an image when backlit. Shade usually a cylinder or a polygon of flat panels.
modular / stackable
Shade printed as rings or tiers that stack. Each tier joint is its own small mount.
☾ silhouettes.txt
2 · Overall shape

Shade silhouettes

drum / cylinder
Straight vertical sides, same top and bottom diameter.
empire
Narrow top, wide bottom, straight sloping sides. The classic table lamp shade.
coolie / cone
Very narrow top, very wide bottom. Throws light down.
bell
Sides curve outward at the bottom like a bell.
dome
Half-sphere cap. Common for pendants and mushroom lamps.
globe / sphere
Fully round with openings at top and bottom for the socket and heat.
hourglass / waisted
Pinched in the middle. Prints well because the overhangs stay gentle.
vase / urn
Organic bulge with a narrower neck. Good fit for vase-mode printing.
lofted profile
Shape blended between two or more cross-sections, such as a circle at the bottom and a hexagon at the top.
revolved profile
A 2D curve spun around the center axis. Describe it with points: "base Ø120, widest Ø160 at 80 mm, top Ø90".
polygonal / n-gon
Flat sides instead of a circle: "8-sided", "hexagonal".
tapered
Any shade that narrows toward one end. Give the taper angle or both diameters.
☾ surface_patterns.txt
3 · Surface treatment

Surface patterns

Add a number to any of these and you get a different lamp: rib count, twist angle, cell size, amplitude.

Linear and rotational
ribbed / fluted
Vertical ridges (ribs stick out, flutes cut in). Key params: rib count, rib depth.
twisted rib / helical twist
Ribs that rotate as they rise. Key param: twist angle top to bottom, e.g. 90° or 180°.
pleated
Sharp zig-zag folds like a paper lampshade.
wave / sinusoidal
Surface rippled by a sine wave. Params: amplitude, frequency, vertical or horizontal.
stacked rings / tiered
Horizontal bands or discs with gaps. Lets light escape sideways.
louvered / slatted
Angled horizontal fins that block direct glare.
spiral
One continuous ridge winding up the shade.
Faceted and geometric
faceted / low-poly
Surface made of flat triangles or polygons. Param: facet count.
origami / Miura fold
Folded-paper geometry with alternating peaks and valleys.
diamond / argyle
Crossing diagonal grooves forming diamonds.
Penrose / Islamic tiling
Non-repeating or star-based tile patterns, usually cut through as openings.
Organic and generative
voronoi
Irregular cell pattern like dried mud or leaf veins, cut through the wall. Params: cell count, strut width.
gyroid / TPMS lattice
Smooth, continuous 3D lattice. Diffuses light well and prints without supports.
hex lattice / honeycomb
Hexagonal cells, as openings or as raised texture.
perforated
Grid or scatter of round holes. Params: hole Ø, spacing, gradient.
reaction-diffusion / coral
Organic maze-like growth texture. Usually made in Blender or nTop and merged in.
superformula
One equation that produces flower, star or starfish cross-sections. Good for lofted shades.
knurled / textured
Fine surface texture, mostly for grip or to hide layer lines.
Light behavior
diffuser / opal effect
Solid thin wall that glows evenly. Use natural or white translucent filament.
cut-through vs. relief
Cut-through casts patterns on the walls. Relief keeps the shade solid and glows in bands of thickness.
double-wall / inner diffuser
Patterned outer shell plus a plain inner sleeve to hide the bulb.
lithophane
Image encoded as wall thickness. Thick = dark, thin = bright.
☾ mount_types.exe
4 · How the shade meets the base

Mount types

Think of every mount as an answer to three questions: what motion attaches it (drop, push, twist, slide), what keeps it there (gravity, friction, a hook, a magnet), and what parts are not printed (magnets, screws, a store-bought fitter). Filter by the motion:

Bayonet (twist-lock)

easy printtwist

Lugs on one part drop into L-shaped slots on the other, then a short twist locks them. Same idea as a camera lens or a GU10 bulb.

Holds by
Lug trapped under slot ledge
Params
lug count (2–4), lug size, twist angle (20–45°), radial clearance 0.3–0.5 mm
Upgrade
Add a small detent bump at the end of travel so it clicks

Say: "3-lug bayonet, 30° twist, 0.4 mm clearance, detent click"

Threaded collar (screw-on)

mediumtwist

Shade screws onto the base like a jar lid. Very secure and looks seamless, but needs a coarse thread to print well.

Holds by
Thread engagement
Params
pitch 3–5 mm, trapezoidal or buttress profile, 1.5–3 turns, 0.4 mm clearance, 45° thread flank
Watch
Fine metric threads fail when printed; ask for a "printable coarse thread"

Say: "screw-on collar, 4 mm pitch trapezoidal thread, 2 turns"

Cantilever snap hooks

mediumpush

Flexible arms with a hooked tip bend as you push down, then spring back under a lip. Your "clasp" in its simplest form.

Holds by
Hook under undercut
Params
arm length ≥ 5× thickness, hook depth 0.8–1.5 mm, 30° lead-in, 90° retention face (permanent) or 45° (removable)
Watch
Print arms vertically along layers or they snap at a layer line. PETG flexes better than PLA

Say: "4 cantilever snap hooks, 45° release angle so it can be removed"

Annular snap ring

mediumpush

A continuous rounded bead around the base pops into a matching groove inside the shade. Like a pen cap or a food container lid.

Holds by
Hoop flex over full circumference
Params
bead height 0.3–0.6 mm, thin shade wall at the groove, add 2–4 relief slots to make it flex
Watch
Too stiff in PLA without relief slots

Say: "annular snap fit, 0.5 mm bead, 4 relief slots in collar"

Friction / press fit

easy printpush

A slightly tapered spigot wedges into a collar. Simplest to model, but fit depends heavily on your printer's accuracy.

Holds by
Friction
Params
1–2° taper, 0.1–0.2 mm interference, or add crush ribs (small vertical ribs that deform)
Watch
Loosens with heat cycling. Fine for table lamps, not for pendants

Say: "tapered press fit with 6 crush ribs"

Drop-in (gravity seat)

easiestdrop

The shade simply sits on a ledge or rim. Add a key or notch so it can't spin. Great for testing designs fast.

Holds by
Gravity plus locating key
Params
ledge width 3–5 mm, 0.3 mm clearance, 1–3 keys
Watch
Falls off if knocked. Never use for pendants or sconces

Say: "shade rests on a 4 mm ledge, 2 locating keys"

Magnetic

easy printpushhardware

Neodymium disc magnets glued or embedded in pockets on both parts. Satisfying "snap", instant removal.

Holds by
Magnetic pull, plus a lip to stop sliding
Params
magnet Ø × thickness, pocket +0.2 mm, 3–6 magnets, alternate polarity to self-align
Tip
"Print-in magnets": pause the print and drop them in so they're fully sealed. Keep magnets away from the hot bulb (they weaken above ~80 °C)

Say: "4× 8×3 mm embedded magnets with pause-at-height pockets"

Spider fitter + harp

easy printdrophardware

The traditional lamp method. A wire harp clips under the socket, the shade's spokes ("spider") sit on top, and a decorative finial screws down to hold it.

Holds by
Finial thread on harp stud
Params
harp height (sets shade height), washer hole ~Ø10.5 mm for ¼-27 harp stud
Why
Lets you use any store-bought harp and lets the shade sit well above the bulb

Say: "spider fitter hub for a 7-inch harp, 3 spokes"

Uno / washer fitter (socket ring)

easy printdrophardware

The shade has a center ring that drops over the socket and is clamped by the threaded shade ring that comes with many sockets. Standard on pendants and small table lamps.

Holds by
Clamped between socket and threaded ring
Params
ring hole sized to your socket's threaded sleeve (often ~Ø30–40 mm, measure), 3–4 spokes
Best for
Pendants, where the shade must not let go

Say: "uno fitter ring, Ø__ mm hole for E26 shade ring, 4 spokes"

Bulb clip

easy printpushhardware

A wire clip squeezes onto the bulb and the small shade hangs from it. Used on candelabra and chandelier mini shades.

Holds by
Spring clip on bulb glass
Params
bulb shape (A19, B10 candle), clip boss in shade top
Watch
Shade sits close to the bulb; LED only

Say: "clip-on mini shade for B10 candle bulb"

Dovetail / French cleat slide

mediumslide

A wedge-shaped rail slides into a matching slot and gravity pulls it tight. The go-to for wall sconces: screw the plate to the wall, slide the shade on.

Holds by
Wedge geometry plus gravity
Params
dovetail angle 60–75°, rail length, 0.3 mm clearance, end stop
Tip
Print the rail lying flat so its angled faces are strong

Say: "sconce back plate with 70° dovetail slide-on shade, bottom stop"

Hook-and-peg / keyhole

easy printdropslide

J-shaped hooks on the shade hang over pegs, a rim, or into keyhole slots. Drop and shift sideways to lock. Good for lanterns and big segmented shades.

Holds by
Hook resting on peg, gravity
Params
hook gap = peg Ø + 0.4 mm, hook wall ≥ 2.4 mm
Variant
Keyhole: big hole to insert a mushroom-head peg, slide into the narrow slot

Say: "3 J-hooks hanging on Ø5 mm pegs around the base rim"

Quarter-turn cam lock

mediumtwist

Like a bayonet, but the lug rides up a ramp that pulls the two parts together as you turn, ending in a click. Tighter, no wobble.

Holds by
Wedge preload plus detent
Params
ramp rise 0.5–1 mm over 60–90°, detent bump 0.3 mm
Best for
Mushroom caps and any shade that must not rattle

Say: "quarter-turn cam lock with ramped lugs and detent"

Sleeve + set screw

easy printslidehardware

A sleeve slides along a pipe or stem and a thumb screw clamps it. Lets you adjust shade height. Common on floor and desk lamps.

Holds by
Screw clamping force
Params
bore = pipe OD + 0.3 mm, M4 heat-set insert, knurled thumb screw
Tip
Heat-set brass inserts give printed parts real metal threads

Say: "sliding sleeve on Ø10 mm lamp pipe, M4 heat-set insert, thumb screw"

Pendant cord grip

mediumhardware

For hanging lamps. The cord, not the plastic, carries the weight through a strain relief grip, and the shade is clamped by an uno ring or socket collar.

Holds by
Strain-relief grip plus socket ring
Params
grip thread (often 1/8 IPS), cord OD, shade weight
Rule
Never let a printed snap be the only thing holding a pendant over someone's head

Say: "pendant shade, top hole for 1/8 IPS cord grip, uno ring clamp"

How to think about it

Choosing a mount

Start from the lamp form, then pick by what you care about most.

Lamp formLoad on jointGood picksAvoid
Table / bedsideCompressionBayonet, drop-in with key, magnetic, spider fitterNothing, really. Choose for looks
MushroomCompression + tippingQuarter-turn cam, threaded, bayonet with detentPlain drop-in
PendantTensionUno fitter, cord grip, threaded collarFriction fit, magnets, drop-in
Wall sconceShearDovetail slide, keyhole, hook-and-pegFriction fit
Floor lampCompression + leverageSpider fitter + harp, sleeve + set screwWeak snaps on big shades
Stackable / modularCompression per tierDrop-in with keys, bayonet, magnetsThreads (alignment of pattern drifts)
Need no tools, no extra parts?
Bayonet, snap hooks, drop-in, dovetail.
Want to swap shades often?
Magnetic, bayonet, removable snap (45° face).
Want the joint invisible?
Threaded collar, annular snap, internal bayonet.
Pattern must line up after closing?
Bayonet or keyed drop-in. They stop at a fixed angle; threads don't.
☾ socket_stuff.txt
5 · The part that must fit exactly

Socket & electrical terms

E26 / E27
Standard medium screw base. E26 in North America, E27 in Europe. 26 or 27 mm thread diameter.
E12 / E14
Candelabra base. E12 in North America, E14 in Europe.
GU10 / G9
Twist-lock and pin bases for small spot or capsule bulbs.
A19, G25, ST64, B10
Bulb shapes: standard, globe, Edison-style, candle. Sets the shade's inner clearance.
keyless socket
Socket with no switch on it. Use with an inline cord switch.
socket shell / cap
Outer body of the socket. The cap's threaded hole mounts onto the lamp pipe.
shade ring / socket ring
Threaded ring on the socket's outer sleeve that clamps a shade or uno fitter.
lamp pipe / threaded nipple
Hollow threaded tube, usually 1/8 IPS (~10.3 mm OD), that connects socket to base. Print a hole for it, don't print it.
cord kit / pendant kit
Pre-wired socket, cord, plug and switch. Buy UL-listed.
strain relief
Knot, grip or clamp so pulling the cord doesn't pull the wires off the socket.
harp / finial
Wire frame above the bulb and the decorative screw that holds a spider-fitter shade.
cord channel / wire chase
Internal path for the cord through the base. Size ≥ cord OD + 1 mm with smooth bends.
felt pad / weight pocket
Recess in the base bottom for felt or steel washers so tall lamps don't tip.
Fixed rules for the agent

Measure your actual socket with calipers and hard-code those numbers. Use LED bulbs only. Keep ≥25 mm between bulb and shade, with an opening at the top for heat. Print the socket holder in PETG or ASA, not PLA. Never print the electrical socket itself.

6 · Making it printable

Print terms to put in the prompt

clearance / tolerance
Gap between mating parts. 0.2 mm tight, 0.3–0.4 mm normal, 0.5 mm loose.
wall thickness
Use multiples of nozzle width: 0.8, 1.2, 1.6 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle.
vase mode / spiralize
One continuous wall, no seams, very fast. Shade only: no holes or internal parts.
overhang ≤ 45°
Anything flatter needs supports. Ask for "support-free geometry".
chamfer vs. fillet
Use chamfers on bottom edges (print cleanly), fillets on top edges.
print orientation
Which face is on the bed. Shades usually print upside down (wide rim on the bed).
split for printing
Divide large shades into segments with alignment pins or dovetails.
build volume
Your printer's max size, e.g. 256×256×256 mm. Tell the agent so it splits parts if needed.
first-layer elephant's foot
Squished first layer makes parts bigger at the bottom. Add a 0.4 mm chamfer on mating edges.
test coupon / fit test
Small print of only the joint to tune clearance before the full lamp.
PLA / PETG / ASA
PLA: easy, softens ~60 °C. PETG: tougher, more flexible, ~80 °C. ASA: heat and UV resistant.
natural / translucent filament
Unpigmented filament that glows. White PLA diffuses; black blocks light completely.
heat-set insert
Brass threaded insert melted into a printed hole. Use wherever a screw goes.
watertight / manifold
Mesh with no holes or flipped faces. Required for a clean slice.
